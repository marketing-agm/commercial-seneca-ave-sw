Dear Thomas Family Properties, LLC,

Thank you for the opportunity to submit a proposal for Management Services for 838 Seneca Ave SW.

While the building is vacant, AGM will provide a focused interim scope designed to protect the asset and keep it ready to lease. This includes routine on-site security and condition checks, oversight of utilities and building systems, fire sprinkler monitoring and inspections, and coordination of vendors for grounds, landscaping, and repairs. During the vacancy, management is billed at $750 per month. Once the building is leased, the fee becomes 3% of gross rents, subject to a $1,500 monthly minimum, so the cost of management scales with the income the property generates.

AGM’s management approach is grounded in the protection and growth of invested capital. We operate each asset with fiduciary discipline by controlling operating expenses, providing transparent monthly financial reporting through our Owner Portal, and preserving physical condition through proactive maintenance and capital planning. The result for Ownership is a well-maintained asset, predictable costs, and a single accountable point of contact.

Protecting the Exit

|Sale or Purchase Option

If the tenant holds a purchase option, the tenant may control the timing of the exit. Ownership still controls the record that supports the price, and a complete record shortens diligence and reduces price adjustments and holdbacks.

Books a Buyer Can Rely On
Three years of reconciled financials, presented consistently, with invoice-level support. A buyer discounts figures it cannot verify.
A Documented Capital History
Every capital item with scope, contractor, cost, warranty and in-service date. Undocumented work becomes an inspection contingency.
An Ordered Compliance File
The environmental and compliance record assembled continuously rather than reconstructed under a thirty-day diligence period. On an industrial site this is a common cause of a delayed closing.
A Defensible Lease File
Executed lease, amendments, an estoppel-ready abstract, reconciliation history and the payment record. These are the evidence of the income the buyer is purchasing.

Questions for Ownership

|Open Items

AGM needs Ownership’s position on the following before the operating plan is set. They form the agenda for the walk-through proposed under Contact.

01
Intended hold period, given the tenant’s purchase option
If the option is likely to be exercised, the operating plan prepares for a documented handover at the option price. If Ownership intends to hold through the option window, the plan works toward income durability and building condition. AGM needs Ownership’s current view, because the two produce different decisions on most capital items.
02
Distribution frequency and reserve level
Monthly, quarterly or on request. AGM will recommend a reserve level against the five-year capital plan. The balance between cash distributed and cash held is Ownership’s to set, and AGM administers it from there.
03
Capital items over the next five years
Roof, HVAC, paving and electrical service are the main items on a building of this age and type. AGM will inventory remaining useful life in the first ninety days. AGM needs to know what Ownership is already aware of and what it is prepared to fund.
04
Environmental budget and approval
AGM proposes an annual budget line with a standing approval threshold for routine monitoring and reporting, and Ownership approval above it. AGM needs the current obligations, the consultant of record and last year’s cost.
05
Spending authority
AGM proposes [$X] per item for unbudgeted spending, with emergency authority limited to preventing injury or further damage and same-day notice to Ownership. Ownership sets both figures in the agreement.
06
Reporting distribution
Accountant, attorney, lender and any family members with an interest. Each can be given portal access at the level Ownership chooses.
07
Leasing and sale brokerage
If a listing broker is already engaged, the management agreement carves out leasing and sales and defines how AGM supports that broker. AGM does not require the brokerage to take the management.
